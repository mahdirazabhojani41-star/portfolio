★ HERO PROJECT • COMPUTER VISION & AI COACH

RepRight

“Don't just count reps. Perfect every rep.”

THE PROBLEM

Improper lifting mechanics — particularly in barbell squats — lead to chronic lumbar strain and severe knee injuries. Gym-goers often train alone without real-time, objective feedback on whether their hips break parallel or if their spine remains neutral.

THE AI SOLUTION

An intelligent camera fitness coach running directly in the browser. Using computer vision pose landmark estimation, RepRight tracks 33 anatomical keypoints in real time, calculates trigonometric joint angles, validates parallel squat depth, tallies reps automatically, and delivers instantaneous corrective voice and visual cues.

MY ROLE & SCOPE

Designed, built, and deployed the full application on Vercel. Implemented the landmark detection pipeline, angle computation mathematics, state-machine rep counter, and low-latency camera rendering. Started squat-only; currently expanding the engine to deadlifts, bench press, and overhead push movements.
